# Brazilian E-Commerce — Power BI Dashboard

An end-to-end **Power BI e-commerce analytics project** built to analyze Brazilian e-commerce performance using transactional data. The project covers data preparation, dimensional modeling, KPI development, and interactive dashboard design.

### 🔹 Project Overview

The dashboard provides insights across **Executive Performance, Customer Analytics, RFM & Cohort Analysis, Sales & Regional Performance, and Delivery Efficiency**.

### 🔹 Key Metrics & KPIs

**💰 Revenue & Sales**

* Total Revenue & Delivered Revenue
* GMV & Delivered GMV
* Revenue / GMV Loss %
* Average Order Value (AOV)
* Revenue per Customer & Seller
* Revenue Growth & YoY Growth
* Freight Revenue & Freight Cost Ratio
* Revenue & Orders by State and Category

**👥 Customer Analytics**

* Total & New Customers
* Repeat Customers & Repeat Purchase Rate
* Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
* Average Orders per Customer
* Average Review Score & NPS-like Metrics
* Positive Review Rate
* Customer Recency, Frequency & Monetary Value

**🎯 RFM & Cohort Analysis**

* RFM Scores & Customer Segmentation
* Recency, Frequency & Monetary Scores
* First Purchase Date
* Cohort Index
* Customer Cohort Performance

**📦 Orders & Operations**

* Total Orders & Order Status Distribution
* Approval, Shipping & Delivery Times
* Cancellation / Unavailability Rate
* Delivery Accuracy
* Late Delivery Rate
* Delivery Efficiency Over Time

**🚚 Delivery & Logistics**

* Shipping Cost Ratio
* Shipping Cost by State
* Late Delivery by State
* Delivery Time by State
* Longest Delivery States

### 🔹 Data & Modeling

* **Data Wrangling:** Python & Power Query
* **Data Transformation:** Cleaning, filtering, merging, handling missing values, and feature engineering
* **Data Modeling:** Designed and implemented a **Star Schema** with fact and dimension tables
* **DAX:** Developed calculated measures, KPIs, growth calculations, cohort analysis, and RFM segmentation
* **Visualization:** Built an interactive Power BI dashboard with KPI cards, filters, time-series analysis, geographic analysis, and drill-down capabilities

### 🔹 Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI**
* **DAX**
* **Power Query / M**
* **Python**
* **Pandas**
* **Star Schema / Dimensional Modeling**

### 🔹 Project Workflow

**Raw Data → Python & Power Query → Data Cleaning & Transformation → Star Schema → DAX Measures → Power BI Dashboard → Business Insights**

The goal of this project is to demonstrate an end-to-end **Business Intelligence workflow**, from raw data preparation and dimensional modeling to interactive reporting and KPI-driven decision making.
